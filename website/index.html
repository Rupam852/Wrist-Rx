Privacy Policy
Wrist Rx Local Data & Encryption Safeguards

At Wrist Rx, your privacy and health telemetry security are our highest priority.

1. Local On-Device Storage

Your smartwatch heart rate, pedometer step data, and emergency contacts are stored locally on your mobile device using encrypted SecureStorage technology. We do not sell or monetize personal health telemetry.

2. Bluetooth & Location Permissions

Bluetooth (BLE) permissions are used strictly to discover and receive sensor packets from your paired smartwatch. Location access is required by Android OS for BLE scanning and attaching precise GPS coordinates during emergency SOS alerts.

3. Sync on Cloud Feature

When you manually enable "Sync on Cloud", your profile and emergency contact settings are backed up to our cloud server to ensure instant recovery across your devices.
