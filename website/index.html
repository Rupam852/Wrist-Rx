FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Wrist Rx health tracking and emergency protection.

What smartwatch hardware is Wrist Rx built for?
Wrist Rx is custom-engineered to seamlessly pair with our upcoming Wrist Rx Smartwatch hardware prototype, while maintaining universal BLE 5.0 GATT standard health service compatibility.
How does the 1-Tap SOS Emergency feature work?
When triggered, SOS activates a loud warning siren to alert people nearby and automatically dispatches emergency SMS messages containing your real-time Google Maps GPS coordinates to your emergency contacts.
Is my health telemetry data kept private?
100% Yes. Your heart rate telemetry, pedometer logs, and contact list remain encrypted on your local mobile device using SecureStorage. We never sell or track your personal health data.
Does Wrist Rx work offline without internet?
Yes! Bluetooth smartwatch telemetry, hybrid pedometer tracking, and SOS Emergency SMS work completely offline. Internet is only required when chatting with Gemini AI or enabling Cloud Sync backup.

Privacy Policy
Wrist Rx Local Data & Encryption Safeguards

At Wrist Rx, your privacy and health telemetry security are our highest priority.

1. Local On-Device Storage

Your smartwatch heart rate, pedometer step data, and emergency contacts are stored locally on your mobile device using encrypted SecureStorage technology. We do not sell or monetize personal health telemetry.

2. Bluetooth & Location Permissions

Bluetooth (BLE) permissions are used strictly to discover and receive sensor packets from your paired smartwatch. Location access is required by Android OS for BLE scanning and attaching precise GPS coordinates during emergency SOS alerts.

3. Sync on Cloud Feature

When you manually enable "Sync on Cloud", your profile and emergency contact settings are backed up to our cloud server to ensure instant recovery across your devices.
